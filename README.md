# Biogas Purification, Upgrading and Compression for Rural Transformation

## 🌱 Project Overview


This project demonstrates a biogas purification and upgrading system that converts organic waste, such as cow dung and agricultural waste, into cleaner fuel. It focuses on removing impurities like hydrogen sulphide (H₂S), carbon dioxide (CO₂), and moisture from raw biogas.

The purified biogas can be used for cooking, electricity generation, and other rural energy applications. The project supports the theme **“Science for Rural Transformation”** by promoting renewable energy and sustainable waste management.

## 🎯 Objectives

- To produce biogas from organic waste.
- To demonstrate the purification of raw biogas.
- To understand the removal of H₂S, CO₂, and moisture.
- To explore biogas compression and storage.
- To promote clean and affordable energy in rural areas.
- To use leftover slurry as organic fertilizer.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

The project works on the principle of anaerobic digestion, in which microorganisms break down organic waste in the absence of oxygen and produce biogas.

The raw biogas is then passed through different purification stages to improve its quality.

## 🔬 Procedure

1. **Biogas Production:** Cow dung and water are mixed and placed in an airtight digester to produce biogas through anaerobic digestion.
2. **H₂S Removal:** The gas passes through an iron oxide filter to remove hydrogen sulphide.
3. **Moisture Removal:** A moisture trap removes water vapour from the gas.
4. **CO₂ Removal:** A suitable purification system, such as water scrubbing, is used to reduce carbon dioxide.
5. **Gas Testing:** The purified gas is tested for quality and composition.
6. **Compression:** In commercial Compressed Biogas (CBG) plants, purified gas is compressed using specialized equipment.
7. **Storage and Utilization:** The upgraded gas is stored in certified pressure vessels and can be used as a clean energy source.

## 📏 Pressure Requirement

For commercial vehicle-fuel CBG applications, biomethane is generally compressed to approximately **200–250 bar (20–25 MPa)**.

**Note:** This pressure is for industrial CBG systems only. The school demonstration model must use unpressurized containers and must not attempt high-pressure compression.

## 🌍 Applications

- Rural cooking fuel.
- Electricity generation.
- Agricultural waste management.
- Organic fertilizer production.
- Clean transportation fuel (after upgrading to required standards).

## 🌿 Advantages

- Reduces organic waste.
- Produces renewable energy.
- Reduces dependence on fossil fuels.
- Supports sustainable agriculture.
- Helps reduce environmental pollution.
- Encourages rural development.

## 🔭 Future Scope

The system can be developed into a larger biogas purification plant for villages. With improved purification technology and safe storage facilities, it can support rural households, farms, and small industries.

## 🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how scientific technology can transform agricultural waste into useful renewable energy. It provides an eco-friendly approach to waste management, energy conservation, and rural development.
